# Version 2.0.1 — Deprecation & Removal of SDXL ConvRot NVFP4 Support

This release officially deprecates and removes all support and documentation references for **SDXL ConvRot NVFP4** across Stable-Diffusion-WebUI-Forge-Nunchaku, in strict alignment with upstream updates to [Hybrid-Sensitivity-Weighted-Quantization (HSWQ)](https://github.com/ussoewwin/Hybrid-Sensitivity-Weighted-Quantization) and the [ComfyUI-HSWQ-Loader-and-Tools](https://github.com/ussoewwin/ComfyUI-HSWQ-Loader-and-Tools) ecosystem.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Affected Scope** | SDXL (Stable Diffusion XL 1.0 / Illustrious-XL / Animagine-XL architectures) |
| **Deprecated Format** | **SDXL ConvRot NVFP4** (4-bit FP4 weight quantization with simulated / native NVFP4 scales) |
| **Active Standard** | **ConvRot INT8 (reverse hybrid)** strictly (`int8` / `int8 (fp16 LoRA)`) |
| **Core Technical Rationale** | Deterministic latent trajectory divergence: 4-bit precision fundamentally breaks trajectory fidelity in SDXL's high-capacity Cross-Attention UNet (cosine < 0.90, frequent trajectory bifurcation / collapse) |
| **Measurement Insight** | Legacy decoded SSIM (~0.95) was an artifact of VAE smoothing (VAE masking illusion), concealing catastrophic latent-space distribution distortion |
| **Upstream Alignment** | Fully synchronized with HSWQ v2.0+ specifications, removing dead loader codepaths and aligning documentation |

## 2. Technical Background & Root Cause Analysis

### 2.1 Latent Trajectory Divergence vs. Decoded SSIM Illusion (The VAE Masking Trap)
In conventional diffusion model quantization benchmarks, model fidelity was frequently assessed by decoding generated latents into pixel space and calculating Structural Similarity (SSIM) or Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio (PSNR) against an unquantized FP16 baseline. 

Under these legacy pixel-space metrics, SDXL NVFP4 appeared to achieve acceptable SSIM scores (~0.94 - 0.95). However, rigorous investigation using the **deterministic 25-seed latent-trajectory comparison** (`sdxl/diag_impact_sdxl.py` / `benchmark_sdxl_int8.md`) revealed a fundamental flaw:
- **The VAE Masking Illusion**: The SDXL VAE decoder acts as a heavy nonlinear low-pass filter and semantic reconstructor. Severe distribution collapse and structural deviation in latent space are artificially smoothed out during decoding, giving a false impression of fidelity.
- **Latent-Space Reality**: When measuring the per-step latent tensor trajectory cosine similarity:
  $$\text{Cosine}(z_t^{\text{quant}}, z_t^{\text{fp16}}) = \frac{\langle z_t^{\text{quant}}, z_t^{\text{fp16}} \rangle}{\|z_t^{\text{quant}}\|_2 \|z_t^{\text{fp16}}\|_2}$$
  SDXL NVFP4 models consistently failed to reach the production threshold of $\ge 0.95$. In extensive multi-seed benchmarks, the mean final-step cosine similarity dropped below $0.90$, with high rates of latent bifurcation (where the denoising path branches into an entirely distorted semantic basin).

### 2.2 SDXL Cross-Attention UNet Vulnerability under 4-Bit Precision
The architectural complexity of SDXL exacerbates the limitations of 4-bit representation:
1. **Dynamic Dynamic-Range in Cross-Attention Layers**: SDXL utilizes dual text encoders (CLIP ViT-L and OpenCLIP ViT-bigG) feeding 2048-dimensional pooled and sequence embeddings into deep Cross-Attention blocks. The dynamic range of projection matrices (`to_q`, `to_k`, `to_v`, `to_out.0`) in middle and deeper blocks exhibits extreme kurtosis. Uniform or block-wise 4-bit floating-point (E2M1 NVFP4) quantization introduces quantization noise that exceeds the error tolerance of the attention score softmax.
2. **Cascading Non-Linear Error Amplification**: Diffusion models operate as iterative dynamical systems. Unlike one-pass autoregressive LLMs, early-step quantization perturbations in SDXL's UNet compound exponentially across subsequent reverse diffusion steps, irreversibly destabilizing the denoising trajectory.

## 3. SDXL Precision Policy: ConvRot INT8 Reverse Hybrid Standard

To guarantee uncompromising generation quality and zero latent trajectory bifurcation, SDXL quantization is strictly standardized on **ConvRot INT8 (reverse hybrid)**.

### 3.1 Reverse Hybrid Mechanism
Rather than attempting to quantize the entire network and selectively protect a small subset of "important" layers, the HSWQ Reverse Hybrid methodology begins with the full unquantized FP16 baseline and proceeds via trajectory-guided impact minimization:
1. **Sampling-Trajectory Impact Measurement**: The actual impact of quantizing each candidate layer is measured directly on the active sampling trajectory under production sampling schedules (Euler, DPM++ 2M Karras).
2. **Ascending Impact Quantization**: Only the $K$ lowest-impact layers are packed as FULL ConvRot INT8 (`int8_tensorwise`), while all sensitive, structural, and attention-critical layers remain strictly in FP16.
3. **Production Gate**: The resulting model must pass the deterministic 25-seed latent-trajectory gate:
   - **Mean Final Latent Cosine Similarity**: $\ge 0.95$
   - **Latent Bifurcation Count**: **0 / 25 seeds** (zero structural failures)

### 3.2 Comparison Matrix: SDXL Quantization Approaches

| Precision Format | Bit Width | Latent Trajectory Cosine | 25-Seed Bifurcation Rate | Production Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **FP16 / BF16** | 16-bit | 1.0000 (Reference) | 0 / 25 | Baseline Reference |
| **ConvRot INT8 (Reverse Hybrid)** | 8-bit hybrid | **$\ge 0.950$ (avg 0.96+)** | **0 / 25 (0%)** | **Production Standard** |
| **Native All-INT8** | 8-bit uniform | 0.935 - 0.945 | 3 - 5 / 25 (12-20%) | Deprecated / Non-Compliant |
| **ConvRot NVFP4** | 4-bit | **< 0.900** | **12+ / 25 (> 48%)** | **Officially Deprecated & Removed** |

## 4. Repository & Runtime Changes

### 4.1 Documentation & Specification Cleanup
- **`README.md` and `zhmd/README.md`**: Removed all references, feature bullets, and architectural claims regarding SDXL NVFP4 from both the Forge core and vendored `modules/ComfyUI-HSWQ-Loader-and-Tools`.
- **`Changelog/CHANGELOG.md` & `zhmd/CHANGELOG.md`**: Added Version 2.0.1 documentation with language-matched navigation links.

### 4.2 WebUI Forge Loader & Model Handling
- SDXL checkpoints loaded via Forge and ComfyUI-HSWQ-Loader are cleanly routed to FP16 or ConvRot INT8 pipelines.
- Elimination of obsolete NVFP4 scale calculation overhead and fallback branches for SDXL architectures.
